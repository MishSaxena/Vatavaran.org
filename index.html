About Vatavaran

We encourage people to plant a native tree on their birthday. Pledge, plant, and get a downloadable certificate you can print or share.

Why pledge?

Birthdays are personal milestones — turning them into planting days helps our planet and creates a living legacy you can watch grow.

  • Quick record of your pledge
  • Printable certificate for remembrance
  • Simple tips for planting and care

How it works

  1. Fill in the pledge form with your name and birthday.
  2. Choose a tree type and location (optional).
  3. Download your certificate and plant on your birthday — or share with a friend.

Planting & care tips

Simple steps to make sure your tree thrives.

🌱

Choose native

Native trees support local wildlife and require less maintenance.

💧

Water well

Water deeply for the first two years — it's better than frequent shallow watering.

🪵

Protect & mulch

Add mulch and a small fence for protection from pets or rodents.
